The Calendar page overflowed at the bottom: a striped bar between the month
grid and the row of monthly stats, reading "BOTTOM OVERFLOWED BY 46 PIXELS" on
a macOS debug build. In release the stripes go and the content is still
clipped.

Two boxes on the page were sized as a fraction of the window height, and both
were wrong for the same reason -- the number was never the height of the box's
contents, and the app bar and navigation rail take their share of the window
before the body sees any of it.

- The month grid had 57% of the window. TableCalendar's height is its header,
  its day-of-week strip and four to six week rows; a six row month overflowed
  and a five row month fitted, which is what made it look intermittent. It is
  already inside a SingleChildScrollView, so the box bought nothing and is
  gone.
- The sheet a day opens had 37.5%, holding a row of cards as tall as a poster,
  an episode badge and a delete button. That box could not simply go -- the
  Expanded inside it needs a bounded height -- so the sized ListView became a
  horizontally scrolling Row, which measures the cards. Verified: the sheet is
  316pt at every window height, where it used to be 225 at 600 and 375 at
  1000.

test/calendar_layout_test.dart covers both. Six of its seven cases fail on the
unfixed page.

Left alone and filed as issue #122: lib/friends_calendar.dart is a near copy
with both defects, 15 more sites share the idiom, and the monthly stats row
may overflow horizontally in Spanish.
